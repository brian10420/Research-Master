# Collaboration proposal — A daily four-minute stretching routine and self-reported lower-back discomfort among warehouse pickers

Submitted to: Greywick Depot
Submitted by: Marlow University — M. Okafor, Assistant Professor, Department of Occupational Health
Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]

## 1. Summary

This proposal asks Greywick Depot to host the study described below. The study will test whether a supervised four-minute stretching routine at the start of each shift reduces self-reported weekly lower-back discomfort scores compared with usual practice. It will involve 48 participants over 12 weeks. Participation is voluntary and may be withdrawn at any time without consequence for the participant's employment.

## 3. Aim

The study will test whether a supervised four-minute stretching routine at the start of each shift reduces self-reported weekly lower-back discomfort scores compared with usual practice.

## 4. Design

Participants: 48 (24 per arm), recruited at 1 site(s).
Duration: 12 weeks from first enrolment to last follow-up.
Primary outcome: the discomfort score on the site's existing weekly wellbeing survey, recorded weekly.
Allocation: by shift team (cluster allocation).
