Claim: `RESEARCH_STATE.md:39-40` — "The pre-registered margin (1.0) is met and the Welch t-test gives

Evidence (recomputed on the honest means): difference 1.87, se 0.514, Welch df 2.97, t_crit(0.975) =

tested claim, and the data cannot exclude a true gain below 1.0.

1.0 as a non-decisive secondary (free).
